## Who we are

Mogify Studio is a creative and digital product studio based in Karachi, Pakistan, founded by **Muhammad Moiz Ali**. We design brand identities, websites, logos, app interfaces, motion and 3D animation for startups and growing businesses, and we make them impossible to ignore.

## What we do

- **Web design & development**: fast, responsive, hand-coded websites, built mobile-first and tuned for search
- **Brand identity & logo design**: logos, visual identity systems, brand strategy and packaging
- **UI/UX & digital product design**: app and product interfaces, design systems
- **Motion design & 3D animation**: logo animation, brand films, promos and social reels
- **Social media & ad creatives**: campaigns and posts that stop the scroll
- **Book & e-book cover design**
